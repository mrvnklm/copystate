Give your AI the page as you see it

Copystate copies the current page as Markdown: text, tables, links and every form field with its value and the options of its dropdowns, including custom ones. Paste it into any AI assistant and ask about the form you are filling in.

Firefox Chrome: in review Try it on the demo page

How it works

1. OpenClick the toolbar icon or press Alt+Shift+M (Option+Shift+M on macOS). Copystate reads the page and shows a preview with fields, characters and a rough token count. Nothing on the page is touched and nothing is copied yet.
2. DropdownsSome dropdowns only show their options while open. If the page has such dropdowns, Open dropdowns opens each one briefly and closes it again. Nothing gets selected or submitted.
3. CopyClick Copy or press Enter. The Markdown is on your clipboard.

Privacy

Copystate runs entirely in your browser. It sends nothing anywhere: no network requests, no analytics, no account. Form fields for passwords, card numbers, IBANs, one-time codes and tokens are redacted, as are credentials and tokens in link URLs. Details: privacy policy.
